How to read this page

  1. Click any card or row to open the drill-down. Every scored metric shows its full arithmetic — raw → winsorized → shrunk → percentile × weight = contribution — so a rank can be checked, not just read.
  2. Read the band before the rank. People in the same tie band are statistically indistinguishable. This data supports 3 bands, not a 1-to-50 ordering — #4 is not meaningfully ahead of #9 if they share a band.
  3. Open ⓘ how on any metric to see how it is computed, including what was excluded from the denominator. A number whose denominator is hidden cannot be argued with.
  4. Check the evidence links. They deliberately favour PRs that explain a bad score — the reverted change, the 4,000-line diff, the approval with no comment. Those are the ones worth discussing.
  5. Scored vs Context. Only 9 metrics affect the score. The other 24 — PR count, cycle time, size — are shown because they are informative, and carry zero weight because they reward volume.
